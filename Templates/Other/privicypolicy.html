Privacy and Account Controls

This page describes the controls in this website. Contact support@themultitools.xyz with support questions. For direct contact and privacy requests, email contact@themultitools.xyz.

Accounts and security

Firebase Authentication handles email/password and Google sign-in. MultiTools profiles store your email, chosen username, account UID, registration date, verification status and last sign-in sync. New security events record the UID, action and time. New code does not collect IP addresses or device fingerprints; historical records may contain those fields. Authorized administrators can access profile information within their assigned permissions.

Email choices

Marketing is optional and the signup choice starts unchecked. Registration alone does not subscribe you. Settings let you opt in or unsubscribe. Preferences record the address, choice, date, source and notice version. Account verification and password reset messages are separate. No marketing email service is currently configured.

Browser storage and optional services

Essential browser storage supports your requested sign-in session, timer state and privacy choices. Cookie Settings can enable tool statistics or external services separately; both start off. Tool statistics count signed-in tool opens in Firestore and load popular-tool counts. External services contact the MultiTools Statuspage service and, when you use that tool, the No as a Service provider. A request exposes network information such as your IP address to its recipient. Fonts, icons and application scripts are served with this site. No Google Analytics script is included.

Your versioned choices are stored in this browser for 180 days. Use the footer Cookie Settings control to change them. Withdrawal stops new optional activity and cancels pending optional service fetches where possible. It cannot undo a request already completed or remove previously recorded statistics.

Your data and requests

In Privacy and Data settings, you can download your own profile and email preferences after confirming your identity, or request access, correction, portability, erasure, restriction, objection or consent withdrawal. Available rights depend on applicable law. Requests have a status and assigned handler. We must verify identity before fulfilling requests that disclose or erase data.

Account deletion removes sign-in access, your profile, preferences and username reservation. A tracked case remains for an operator to check historical records, Storage, backups and any connected services. Some steps require manual work, so this control does not promise instant removal of every record. Contact support if you cannot sign in or need a broader data review.

Service providers and external links

The site uses GitHub Pages for hosting and Google Firebase for authentication and database operations. Optional external services are described above. Google sign-in follows Google's own authentication flow. Tools may open websites you choose, such as YouTube or TikTok; those sites have their own policies. Local generators process their inputs in your browser unless the tool describes an external action.
