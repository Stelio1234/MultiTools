Privacy Policy

Welcome to MultiTools. Your privacy matters to us, and we want you to understand what information may be collected when you use our website, how that information is used, and what choices you have regarding your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains how MultiTools handles information collected through https://themultitools.xyz, including account information, authentication, browser storage, optional services and email preferences.

If you have questions about this policy, need assistance or want to make a privacy request, contact us:

  • General support: support@themultitools.xyz
  • Privacy requests and direct contact: contact@themultitools.xyz

1. Information We Collect

The information collected depends on how you use MultiTools. You can browse and use many of our tools without creating an account. Certain features, particularly account-related functionality, require authentication.

Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect and store information including:

  • Email address associated with your account.
  • Your chosen username.
  • Your unique account identifier (UID).
  • Account registration date.
  • Email verification status.
  • Last sign-in synchronization information.
  • Account preferences and settings.
  • Promotional email subscription preferences.

Your username and account UID help us identify and manage accounts. Usernames must be unique, and we do not allow multiple accounts to reserve the same username.

Google Sign-In Information

If you choose to sign in using Google, Google Authentication may provide information associated with your Google account, such as your name, email address and profile picture, depending on the permissions requested and granted.

MultiTools uses Google Firebase Authentication to handle the authentication process. We do not have access to your Google account password or private Google account settings.

Technical and Usage Information

When visiting a website, certain technical information may be processed by the website, hosting provider or external services. This may include:

  • Browser type and version.
  • Device platform and operating system.
  • Screen dimensions and display information.
  • Timezone and language preferences.
  • General network information, including IP addresses where processed by service providers.
  • Website interactions and tool usage, where optional statistics are enabled.

MultiTools does not currently include a Google Analytics tracking script. Optional tool statistics are described in the Browser Storage and Optional Services section below.

Information You Submit

Some tools allow you to enter text, upload files or provide other information. Where a tool processes information locally in your browser, that information is not intentionally transmitted to MultiTools servers by that local processing functionality.

Other tools may communicate with external services to perform their requested functionality. Those services may receive information necessary to process your request. Their own privacy policies and practices apply.

Please avoid entering passwords, financial information, government identification numbers or other sensitive personal information into tools unless the feature specifically requires it.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through MultiTools may be used for legitimate website operations and purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Authenticating users and maintaining sign-in sessions.
  • Providing account-related functionality.
  • Remembering user preferences and settings.
  • Maintaining username uniqueness.
  • Improving website functionality and compatibility.
  • Identifying bugs, technical problems and security issues.
  • Responding to support requests.
  • Sending essential account-related notifications.
  • Sending promotional announcements to users who have opted in.
  • Handling privacy requests and complying with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

We do not use your account information for purposes unrelated to operating, maintaining, securing and improving MultiTools without an appropriate legal basis.

3. Accounts and Authentication Security

MultiTools uses Google Firebase Authentication to provide email/password authentication and Google Sign-In.

Firebase handles authentication credentials and sign-in processes. MultiTools maintains account profile information necessary to operate the website.

MultiTools profiles may contain your email address, username, UID, registration date, verification status and last sign-in synchronization information.

New security events may record your account UID, the action performed and the time of the event to help maintain account security and investigate potential problems.

New MultiTools security-event code does not collect IP addresses or device fingerprints. Historical records may contain these fields.

Authorized administrators may access account information within their assigned permissions when necessary to maintain the service, provide support or address security and operational issues.

We take reasonable measures to protect account information. However, no website, online service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your account and protecting access to your sign-in credentials.

4. Email Communications and Promotional Subscriptions

MultiTools distinguishes between essential account-related emails and optional promotional communications.

Essential Account Emails

We may send emails necessary to provide account functionality, including:

  • Email verification messages.
  • Password reset messages.
  • Important account security notifications.
  • Responses to support requests.
  • Other necessary service-related communications.

These communications are separate from promotional email subscriptions. Disabling promotional emails does not necessarily prevent essential account-related communications.

Optional Promotional Emails

MultiTools offers an optional promotional email subscription. Users can choose whether they want to receive:

  • New feature announcements.
  • Website updates.
  • MultiTools news.
  • Service announcements.
  • Other promotional information about MultiTools.

Promotional email subscriptions are optional. The signup choice starts unchecked, and creating an account does not automatically subscribe you.

Users can subscribe or unsubscribe through their account settings: MultiTools Settings.

Subscription preferences may record the email address, subscription choice, date, source and notice version associated with the preference.

Users can withdraw their consent to promotional emails at any time. Unsubscribing does not affect their ability to use MultiTools.

Promotional email delivery is subject to the availability and configuration of an email delivery service. No marketing email service is currently configured.

We do not treat account registration alone as permission to send promotional emails.

5. Cookies, Browser Storage and Consent Settings

MultiTools uses browser storage and similar technologies to support website functionality and remember certain preferences.

Essential Storage

Essential browser storage supports functionality such as:

  • Maintaining requested sign-in sessions.
  • Saving timer state.
  • Remembering privacy and consent choices.
  • Supporting basic website functionality.

Optional Services

MultiTools provides optional privacy settings that allow users to control certain additional activities.

The optional settings include:

  • Tool Statistics: When enabled, signed-in tool opens may be counted in Firestore and used to display popular-tool counts.
  • External Services: When enabled, the website may communicate with supported external services when you use the relevant functionality.

Both optional settings start disabled.

Your versioned privacy choices are stored in your browser for 180 days. You can change your preferences through the Cookie Settings control in the website footer.

Withdrawing optional consent stops new optional activity and cancels pending optional service requests where technically possible. Withdrawal cannot undo a request that has already been completed or automatically remove statistics that have already been recorded.

Essential browser storage may continue to operate where necessary for requested website functionality.

6. Analytics and Website Usage Statistics

MultiTools may use limited usage statistics to understand how website features are used and identify popular tools.

When optional Tool Statistics are enabled, signed-in tool opens may be recorded in Firestore and used to calculate popular-tool counts.

These statistics help us understand which tools are useful and where improvements may be needed.

MultiTools does not currently include Google Analytics.

Optional statistics are separate from essential website functionality and start disabled.

9. Data Storage, Retention and Deletion

Account and website information may be stored using Google Firebase and other infrastructure providers necessary to operate MultiTools.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce applicable agreements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

Account Deletion

Users can request account deletion through the MultiTools Settings page.

Account deletion removes sign-in access, your profile, email preferences and username reservation.

A tracked deletion case remains so an authorized operator can check historical records, Storage, backups and any connected services.

Some deletion steps require manual work. Therefore, requesting account deletion does not guarantee that every record is immediately removed from every system or backup.

Information may need to be retained for legitimate legal, security or operational reasons where permitted or required by applicable law.

Contact contact@themultitools.xyz if you need assistance with a deletion request or want to ask about information that may remain after account deletion.
